Privacy

The short version: this site can only know what you choose to type into one form, and the one thing that form asks for is an email address.

This site sets no cookies and runs no analytics. The fonts are ours and the images are ours, and no page here phones anywhere to watch you read it. There is no consent banner because there is no tracking to consent to. You can confirm that in your browser's developer tools, and we'd rather you did.

What this site collects

Nothing, until you decide to join the waitlist. If you do, the form sends your email address, and which page you were on when you typed it. There is no second form and no hidden field.

We don't keep it ourselves. The address goes to Resend, the company that sends our email, and the list lives there — we hold no copy of it in our own database. Resend records your address, whether you've confirmed it, and whether you've unsubscribed, which is the whole of what a mailing list is.

What we use it for

{{CTA_SUPPORT}} That's the line under the form, and it's also the whole scope of use. No newsletters you didn't ask for, no surveys, no "we thought you might also like."

What we never do with it

  • Sell it, rent it, or trade it — to anyone, at any size of company.
  • Share it with anyone who wants it for their own purposes. Resend is the one company that touches it, they send only what we ask them to send, and they may not use it for anything else.
  • Enrich it. We don't look up who you are from your address or append data from anywhere.
  • Use it to train anything.

Unsubscribing

Every email has an unsubscribe link, and it works in one click — no login, no survey, no "are you sure." Unsubscribing removes your address; it doesn't move you to a quieter list. Nobody attaches a note about why you left.

One honest detail: unsubscribing keeps your address on a suppression list at Resend. That sounds backwards and isn't — it is how they know never to email you again. Ask us to delete it outright and we will, and then nothing stops a future form from mailing you afresh.

Seeing it, correcting it, deleting it

Email {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}} and a person will handle it — there's no request form and no verification maze, because the only thing there is to hold about you is an email address, and a record of when you gave it. You have these rights whichever privacy law applies to you. We're not going to make you cite one.
