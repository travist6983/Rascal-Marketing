Privacy

The short version: this site can only know what you choose to type into one form, and the one thing that form asks for is an email address.

This site sets no cookies. It runs no analytics. It makes no requests to anyone else's servers — the fonts are ours, the images are ours, and no page here phones anywhere when you load it. That's why there is no consent banner: there is nothing to consent to. You can confirm all of this in your browser's developer tools, and we'd rather you did.

What this site collects

Nothing, until you decide to join the waitlist. If you do, we store what you typed: your email address, plus two separate yes-or-no records — one that says you asked for the daily prompt email, and one that says you want a note when the app opens and when the price goes live. They're kept as two fields, not one flag, so each can be honored on its own later. That's the entire dataset. There is no second form.

What we use it for

{{CTA_SUPPORT}} That's the line under the form, and it's also the whole scope of use. No newsletters you didn't ask for, no surveys, no "we thought you might also like."

What we never do with it

  • Sell it, rent it, or trade it — to anyone, at any size of company.
  • Share it.
  • Enrich it. We don't look up who you are from your address or append data from anywhere.
  • Use it to train anything.

Unsubscribing

Every email has an unsubscribe link, and it works in one click — no login, no survey, no "are you sure." Unsubscribing removes your address; it doesn't move you to a quieter list. We don't keep a file of former subscribers with notes attached.

Seeing it, correcting it, deleting it

Email {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}} and a person will handle it — there's no request form and no verification maze, because the only thing we hold about you is an email address. You have these rights whichever privacy law applies to you. We're not going to make you cite one.
