What {{PRODUCT}} is

{{PRODUCT}} is an iPhone app for keeping a private archive about your kid. It asks you a question about them every day, and it files what you answer — the words, the photographs, the recordings — with the prompt that caused it, so it can still be found in twenty years.

You are the account holder, and you are a parent or guardian. The archive is about your child; the account is yours. Your child does not have a login, and nothing in the app asks them for one. That distinction runs through the whole of this page and the whole of the privacy page, and it's the thing that makes this app unusual enough to say twice.

You need to be old enough to enter a contract where you live, and you need to be a parent or guardian of the child you're keeping the archive about. That's the whole eligibility test.

Cancelling, and why we can't do it for you

Cancellation happens in your Apple Account settings, not in this app. Open the iPhone Settings app, tap your name, then Subscriptions, then {{PRODUCT}}, then Cancel. {{PRODUCT}}'s own Settings screen has a Manage subscription row that opens the same Apple screen for you, so you don't have to go looking — but the screen is Apple's either way, and so is the decision it records. Cancelling takes effect at the end of the period you have already paid for; the membership runs until then.

If your household has two parents on it, that row only appears for the one who bought the membership. Apple ties a subscription to the Apple Account that paid for it, and we can't move it to the other parent — they'd have to cancel and buy their own.

That's the honest answer to most of the questions we get, so here is the rest of it: Apple handles the billing, the renewals, the cancellations and the refunds — not us. We never see your card. We can't charge it, we can't stop it charging, and we can't refund it. Refund requests go to Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com. If you write to us instead, we'll tell you that and help you find the screen — we'd just rather you knew before you needed it than after.

We don't get to make cancelling hard, and we wouldn't want the version of this business that did.

  • Nothing you wrote or captured is ever locked behind payment. Every entry stays readable, searchable and exportable, whether you pay or not.
  • Text never stops. The daily question keeps arriving, and written and did it entries carry on without a limit.

If that ever changes — if we ever need to age out media from archives that have been both unpaid and unopened for years — we will say so on this page and email you before it applies to you, and it will never touch a word you wrote.

Closing your account

You can ask us to close your account from inside the app — Settings, then the card at the bottom. It waits seven days before anything is destroyed, signs you out immediately, and is cancelled outright the moment you sign back in. Somebody who signs back in has changed their mind, and we'd rather read it that way round.

What it removes depends on whether you are the only parent on the archive. If you are, it takes the lot: your account, your children, every entry, and the photographs, videos and recordings themselves out of storage. If your child's other parent is still on the archive, it removes you — your account, your sign-in, your place on the household — and leaves the archive with them. A shared archive isn't one parent's to destroy, and they didn't ask for anything.

One consequence of that worth saying out loud, because it surprises people: in a two-parent household the entries you wrote stay in the archive after you go. Your name comes off them and your account is gone, but the words your child's other parent has been reading for years don't vanish out from under them. If that isn't what you want, the thing to do is talk to them, not to us.

Take the export first if you want your own copy. Once a deletion has run there is nothing left for us to hand back.

One thing we're not going to overstate. This is the newest part of the product, and the piece that carries out the deletion on a schedule is not fully in service yet. Ask us to close your account and the request is filed and honoured — but if you want it confirmed to you in writing, email {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}} and a person will do it by hand and tell you when it's done.
