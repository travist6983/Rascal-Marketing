Why the question is the free part

Because it is the thing that makes you stop and think about your kid for fifteen seconds, and a version of this that charged per question would be a worse product on the day it made the most money. Some apps in this category meter questions per day by tier. {{PRODUCT}} does not, and will not — the daily question arrives every day whether you are a member or not.

The missions are where the money is because that is where the cost is. A mission asks for a photograph, a video, thirty seconds of their voice — files, kept for eighteen years, which is the one part of this that gets more expensive every year you use it.

Why {{FREE_PHOTOS}} photos is a lifetime number

Not per month. A monthly reset lets a free archive grow forever against no revenue, and the honest version of this is a number that doesn't move. A cap can always be raised. It can never be lowered, so it is set where it can stay.

It is also not a countdown. Nothing warns you, nothing turns red, and nothing you have already put in there is touched when you reach it.

If you stop paying

What happens to the archive

Most apps handle this badly, so here it is in full, on the pricing page, before you've given anybody a card number.

Your archive is never locked

If you stop paying, {{PRODUCT}} drops to the free tier. Everything you have written and captured stays exactly where it is — readable, searchable, exportable. The daily question keeps arriving and written and did it entries never stop. The missions go back to being part of membership, and new photo and video uploads pause.

After ninety days, photos get cheaper to store

Original photos move to cold storage. Thumbnails stay instant, search still works, and pulling a full-resolution original back takes about an hour. That's a property of the storage, not a penalty, and it's described that way because that's what it is.

We never delete a word you wrote unless you ask us to.

And if media is ever going to be removed, you get warned three times — ninety days out, thirty days out, seven days out — each with a free one-click export of the whole archive. Sign in once during that window and it's cancelled outright. If we can't reach you, we keep it.

We have never deleted a word a parent wrote.

The export is free on the free tier

A zip of ordinary files in date folders plus an index.html that opens in any browser, offline, with no account. Free, complete, always — even if you stopped paying years ago. The whole promise, including what happens if we go away.
