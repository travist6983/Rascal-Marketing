Why the question is the free part

Because it is the thing that makes you stop and think about your kid for fifteen seconds, and a version of this that charged per question would be a worse product on the day it made the most money. Some apps in this category meter questions per day by tier. {{PRODUCT}} does not, and will not — the daily question arrives every day whether you are a member or not.

The missions are where the money is because that is where the cost is. A mission asks for a photograph, a video, their voice — files, kept for eighteen years, which is the one part of this that gets more expensive every year you use it.

If you stop paying

What happens to the archive

Most apps handle this badly, so here it is in full, on the pricing page, before you've given anybody a card number.

Your archive is never locked

If you stop paying, {{PRODUCT}} drops to the free tier. Everything you have written and captured stays exactly where it is — readable, searchable, exportable. The daily question keeps arriving and written and did it entries never stop. The missions go back to being part of membership.

We never delete a word you wrote unless you ask us to.

Asking us to is the one thing that does delete an archive. It waits seven days, and signing in during that week calls it off.

The export is free on the free tier

A zip of ordinary files in date folders plus an index.html that opens in any browser, offline, with no account. Free, complete, always — even if you stopped paying years ago. The whole promise, including what happens if we go away.

Questions about the money

The rest are on the FAQ.

Is the free tier a trial?

No. It has no end date and no countdown. The daily question arrives every day, forever, and everything you have already written and captured stays yours. The missions are the part that's membership.

What do I have to give you to try the whole thing?

Nothing at all. You get {{TRIAL}} with everything open — every mission, video, the lot — and there's no card, so there's nothing to cancel. When it ends, the daily question carries on, and the missions — with the photographs, video and recordings they ask for — become part of membership.

What happens to the photographs I already have?

They stay exactly where they are — readable, searchable, exportable, on any tier, whether you paid once or never. What membership buys is adding new ones.

Do I pay more for a second child?

No. One membership covers the household — every kid, both parents. Having another child shouldn't cost more.

Where do I buy it?

Inside the app, through the App Store — {{PRODUCT}} is a free download and the daily question needs nothing else. {{PRICE_YEAR}} a year or {{PRICE_MONTH}} a month is the United States price; Apple sets the local equivalent everywhere else, and the figure on the purchase screen is the one that applies. If we ever change it, you'll be told before a renewal is charged at the new one.

How do I cancel?

Settings on your iPhone, two taps, like any other subscription. Apple handles it and we don't get to make it harder. During the {{TRIAL}} there is nothing to cancel, because there is no card on file.

The question is free either way

In the app, every morning, forever — or by email, if you'd rather answer it in a notebook. No card either way.
