If you stop paying

What happens to the archive

Most apps handle this badly, so here it is in full, on the pricing page, before you've given anybody a card number.

Nothing is locked

If you stop paying, {{PRODUCT}} drops to the free tier. Your whole archive stays readable. You keep answering the daily question — text and did-it entries never stop. New photo and video uploads pause.

After ninety days, photos get cheaper to store

Original photos move to cold storage. Thumbnails stay instant, search still works, and pulling a full-resolution original back takes about an hour. That's a property of the storage, not a penalty, and it's described that way because that's what it is.

We never delete a word you wrote unless you ask us to.

And if media is ever going to be removed, you get warned three times — ninety days out, thirty days out, seven days out — each with a free one-click export of the whole archive. Sign in once during that window and it's cancelled outright. If we can't reach you, we keep it.

We have never deleted a word a parent wrote.

The export is free on the free tier

A zip of ordinary files in date folders plus an index.html that opens in any browser, offline, with no account. Free, complete, always — even if you stopped paying years ago. The whole promise, including what happens if we go away.
