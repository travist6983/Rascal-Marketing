{{PRODUCT}} vs Qeepsake

If you're weighing a Qeepsake alternative, start with what Qeepsake gets right. Prompts arrive by text message, and a question you can answer without opening an app is the lowest-friction version of this idea there is. You reply to a text and you're done. That's a real advantage, and a comparison that won't concede it isn't one.

In November 2025, Tinybeans acquired Qeepsake. {{PRODUCT}} vs Tinybeans is its own page.

The short version

Both apps are built on the same observation: a parent who is asked a question writes things down, and a parent who has to think of it themselves mostly doesn't. Past that, the two real differences are where the prompt arrives and where the archive ends up.

Qeepsake sends the question by text and points your answers at a printed book. {{PRODUCT}} asks in an app and points everything at a searchable iPhone archive built to be handed over — the question filed with the moment, so either of you can find it again. Neither is the wrong choice. They're different destinations.

The differences that matter

Where they differ most

Three of those rows do most of the work. Here they are in full sentences.

Prompt volume: metered there, uncapped here

Qeepsake meters how many questions you get by subscription tier — more prompts is a bigger plan. That's a coherent model: the prompts are the product, so the product is what's priced. {{PRODUCT}} prices storage instead. Every question and every mission is free, uncapped, forever, and all {{PROMPT_COUNT}} prompts are published here, free to read, whether you ever install anything or not.

Print-first or archive-first

Qeepsake optimizes toward a finished book — a real object on a real shelf, which is a real thing to want. The trade is that the business runs on the books: the recurring complaints in Qeepsake's reviews are the cost of the books and friction when cancelling. That's the pattern users report, not our judgment of any case. {{PRODUCT}} optimizes toward the archive itself — plain files, search, and an export that opens offline in any browser with no account. No print product exists here yet; that gap is stated plainly below rather than footnoted.

What's saved with a moment

Qeepsake keeps your answer. {{PRODUCT}} keeps the answer and the question that caused it, with the date it happened and anything you captured, on one timeline. That's why search behaves differently here: you aren't searching filenames and dates, you're searching the questions you were asked and the answers you gave. A photo of a half-eaten sandwich is nothing. The same photo filed under the question that caused it is a story.
